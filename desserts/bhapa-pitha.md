Bhapa Pitha is a traditional Bengali steamed rice cake made with rice flour, grated coconut, and sweet jaggery (gur).

Ingredients

• Rice flour: 3 cups (fine store-bought or freshly ground)
• Salt: ½ teaspoon
• Water: About 1 to 1¼ cups of lukewarm water (added gradually)
• Grated coconut: 1 cup
• Jaggery (Khejur'er Gur / Patali gur): ½ finely grated or shaved

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Prepare the Rice Flour:
	• In a large bowl, mix the rice flour with ½ teaspoon of salt.
	• Slowly sprinkle lukewarm water (a few tablespoons at a time) and rub the flour gently with your fingertips.
	• The mixture should be moist enough to hold together when squeezed in your fist, yet crumble easily when poked. It should not turn into a wet dough.
	• Cover and let the mixture rest for 15 minutes.
	• Pass the moistened flour through a fine-mesh strainer or sieve to get a light, sandy, and uniform texture.
2. Assemble the Pitha:
	• Take small small-rimmed molds, small bowls, or specially made pitha cups.
	• Put a loose layer of the sifted rice flour at the bottom (do not pack it down tightly).
	• Add a layer of grated coconut and shaved jaggery in the middle.
	• Cover the filling with another light layer of rice flour on top. Level the surface gently without pressing.
3. Steam the Pitha:
	• Cover the mold with a thin, damp cotton cloth or cheesecloth.
	• Invert or place the mold over a steaming pot of boiling water (or a traditional pitha maker/bamboo steamer).
	• Steam for about 6 to 8 minutes until the rice flour looks opaque, firm, and set.
	• Carefully remove from the heat, peel away the cloth, and serve hot.
